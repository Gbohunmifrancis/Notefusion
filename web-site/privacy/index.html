Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version

Your recordings are not kept. Audio you submit is transcribed and then deleted from our servers, usually within seconds and always as soon as the job finishes. We keep the resulting notation, not the sound.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your recordings or transcriptions to train machine-learning models. Our public web pages show ads from Google; the app and the signed-in web app do not, and nothing you record, save or upload is used for advertising.

Who we are

NoteFusion (“we”, “us”) operates the NoteFusion app and the web app at notefusion.studio. They listen to music, a tune you hum, a recording, or a song you link to, and write it out as Tonic Solfa and staff notation. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the app, the web app, and this website.

Questions or requests: privacy@notefusion.studio.

What we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address.
  • A phone number, only if you choose to add one.
  • A password, stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store, log, or transmit the password itself, and we cannot recover it.

If you sign in with Google

Google sends us your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a stable account identifier. We store these to create and recognise your account. We receive nothing else from your Google account, so no contacts, files or calendar. Google’s own handling of the sign-in is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Accounts created this way have no password on our side.

Audio you record or upload

Audio is sent to our servers, passed to our transcription engine, and deleted once the job completes. It is not archived, not backed up, and not reviewed by a person. Transcription runs on our own infrastructure; your audio is not sent to a third-party AI service.

Songs you link to

When you paste a YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music link, our server fetches the song’s audio, isolates the voice, transcribes it, and deletes the audio the moment the job finishes, exactly as with a recording. We keep the song’s title and artist so the sheet can credit them. The link you pasted is not kept against your account beyond the job.

Transcriptions you save

When you save a result to your library we keep the musical data, which covers note events, detected key, tempo, time signature, solfa labels, MusicXML, any lyrics you type, the alto, tenor and bass parts written for it, and the title you gave it. This is what lets your library persist across the app and the web.

If you share a sheet, we create a link that anyone holding it can open without an account. The page shows the sheet and your name as the person who shared it, and we count how often it is opened. Stop sharing at any time and the link stops working.

A take you sing back

Pro accounts can sing a part and have each note marked. That recording is compared with the sheet and discarded straight away; neither the audio nor the marks are stored.

Profile picture

If you upload one, the image file is stored with our media host, Cloudflare (R2 object storage), and we keep the resulting URL against your account.

Device and technical data

  • A push-notification token per device, if you allow notifications. It tells us where to send a “transcription ready” alert, and nothing more.
  • Sign-in session tokens, so you stay logged in without re-entering credentials.
  • Your approximate country, inferred from your IP address, so the website and app show the pricing available in your region. An anonymous website lookup is used for that response and is not attached to an account.
  • A device identifier, held as a one-way hash, together with the country it was first seen in. It exists so the free allowance (three transcriptions a week) is counted per device as well as per account, which is what stops one device claiming it over and over with new sign-ins. It is not used for advertising and is not shared.
  • For the free 30-second preview on the website, a random identifier your browser keeps and your network address, so the preview is counted per browser and per address. If you then create an account, the preview is attached to it. We also record whether an account was created on the web or in the app, so we can see which parts of the service people find useful.
  • Ordinary server logs, including IP address and error diagnostics, used to keep the service running and to investigate abuse.

Error reports

When something breaks, an error report is sent to Sentry so that we can find and fix it. From the app, a report holds what went wrong in the code, your phone’s model and operating system, the app version, your IP address and your account’s id, with a short screen recording of what led up to the error. The app also records about one session in ten this way, error or not, so we can see where people get stuck. These recordings show the screen as you saw it, including your name, email address, sheets and lyrics; password fields are always blanked, whether hidden or shown. Our team watches them in Sentry and in our admin console, only to fix problems and make the app easier to use. From the web app and our servers, a report holds only what went wrong in the code, which page or request it happened on and, from the web app, your browser’s name and version: no IP address, no email address and no link you pasted. The web app loads Sentry’s code only once an error has happened, to send its report. Sentry keeps reports for at most 90 days.

Payments

Pro is paid for on a checkout page hosted by a payment provider: Paystack for naira, Bachs for US dollars. They receive your email address and the amount, and take your card details themselves; we never see or store card numbers. They tell us whether the payment succeeded, and we keep that record so your plan is right.

Browser storage

Apart from the advertising described below, which runs only on our public pages, the website and web app set no advertising cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in tokens, the preview identifier above, and a few preferences (such as whether sounds are on) in its local storage, for the web app alone. Our website measures page views and clicks with first-party requests to our own server: the page you are on, the site you came from, the label of what you clicked, and two random identifiers the browser keeps in its own storage so that return visits can be counted. We do not record your IP address, browser name or screen size for this, and no third-party analytics scripts run on the site. When you are signed in to the web app, the things you do there (a tune recorded, a sheet saved or shared, a checkout started) are recorded against your account so that we can see how the product is used and support you.

Advertising

Our public pages (the home page and our policy pages at notefusion.studio) show ads served by Google AdSense. The NoteFusion app and the signed-in web app at notefusion.studio/app do not show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through these cookies; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before ads that use cookies are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link on the page. We never pass your account details, recordings or transcriptions to Google for advertising.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Where the UK/EU GDPR applies, our lawful bases are:

  • Performance of a contract, for account data, saved transcriptions, and the processing of audio you submit. Without these the app cannot do what you asked it to do.
  • Consent, for push notifications, microphone access, your profile picture, and advertising cookies on our public pages. Each is optional and each can be withdrawn.
  • Legitimate interests, for security logging and abuse prevention, balanced against your rights.

Who else sees it

We share data only with the providers that run the service:

  • Amazon Web Services: the servers that run the API, the transcription engine and the database.
  • Vercel: hosting of this website and the web app.
  • Cloudflare: profile picture storage and delivery.
  • Resend: the emails we send you, such as the sign-up code and password resets.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications to the app.
  • Google: if you choose Google sign-in, and, through AdSense, the ads on our public pages (see Advertising).
  • Paystack and Bachs: payment, as above.
  • GeoJS: IP-to-country resolution for regional pricing.
  • Sentry: error reports from the app, the web app and our servers (see Error reports).

Each processes data on our instructions, except Google for advertising, which acts as an independent controller as described above. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your account data, recordings or transcriptions for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Audio recordings are deleted as soon as the transcription job finishes.
  • Saved transcriptions and account data stay until you delete them or close your account.
  • Session tokens last until they expire, you sign out, or you change your password.
  • Server logs are kept for a short rolling window, for diagnostics only.

Your choices

In the app, and in the web app’s account settings, you can edit your profile and photo, delete individual transcriptions, stop sharing a sheet, revoke microphone or notification permission at any time through your device or browser settings, and close your account outright.

Closing your account erases your private library. Your profile, your sessions, your registered devices, and everything in your personal library are deleted. This cannot be undone. Because it is irreversible, we ask you to confirm it is really you first, by re-entering your password or by signing in with Google again if that is how you use NoteFusion.

One exception: transcriptions you published to the public African Tunes & Choruses library stay published under your credited name, so the people who saved them keep their copies. Write to privacy@notefusion.studio if you want one removed.

You can also delete your account through the app, from the web app’s account settings, or in a browser via our account deletion page. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export, or restrict the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to privacy@notefusion.studio and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Traffic runs over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Access tokens are short-lived and refresh tokens rotate on every use, so a stolen token has a narrow window. Closing your account or changing your password invalidates existing sessions.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please tell us at security@notefusion.studio before disclosing it publicly.

Children

NoteFusion is not directed at children under 18. If you are 13 to 17, you may use it only with a parent or guardian’s permission, and we will treat that as your consent to our processing. If you are under 13, please do not use the app or give us any personal information.

If you believe a child has given us personal information without the right permission, contact us at privacy@notefusion.studio and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app. Continuing to use NoteFusion after a change means you accept the revised policy.
