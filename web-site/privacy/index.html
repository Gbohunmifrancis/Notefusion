Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version

Your recordings are not kept. Audio you submit is transcribed and then deleted from our servers — usually within seconds, and always as soon as the job finishes. We keep the resulting notation, not the sound.

We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising, and we do not use your recordings or transcriptions to train machine-learning models.

Who we are

NoteFusion (“we”, “us”) provides a mobile app that listens to music and writes it out as Tonic Solfa and staff notation. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Questions or requests: privacy@notefusion.app.

What we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address.
  • A phone number, only if you choose to add one.
  • A password, stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store, log, or transmit the password itself, and we cannot recover it.

If you sign in with Google

Google sends us your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a stable account identifier. We store these to create and recognise your account. We receive nothing else from your Google account — no contacts, no files, no calendar. Google’s own handling of the sign-in is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Accounts created this way have no password on our side.

Audio you record or upload

Audio is sent to our servers, passed to our transcription engine, and deleted once the job completes. It is not archived, not backed up, and not reviewed by a person. Transcription runs on our own infrastructure; your audio is not sent to a third-party AI service.

Transcriptions you save

When you save a result to your library we keep the musical data — note events, detected key, tempo, time signature, solfa labels, MusicXML, and the title you gave it. This is what lets your library persist across devices.

Profile picture

If you upload one, the image file is stored with our media host, Cloudinary, and we keep the resulting URL against your account.

Device and technical data

  • A push-notification token per device, if you allow notifications. It tells us where to send a “transcription ready” alert, and nothing more.
  • Sign-in session tokens, so you stay logged in without re-entering credentials.
  • Ordinary server logs, including IP address and error diagnostics, used to keep the service running and to investigate abuse.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Where the UK/EU GDPR applies, our lawful bases are:

  • Performance of a contract — account data, saved transcriptions, and the processing of audio you submit. Without these the app cannot do what you asked it to do.
  • Consent — push notifications, microphone access, and your profile picture. Each is optional and each can be withdrawn.
  • Legitimate interests — security logging and abuse prevention, balanced against your rights.

Who else sees it

We share data only with the providers that run the service:

  • Heroku (Salesforce) — application hosting and database.
  • Cloudinary — profile picture storage.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.

Each processes data on our instructions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Audio recordings — deleted as soon as the transcription job finishes.
  • Saved transcriptions and account data — until you delete them or close your account.
  • Session tokens — until they expire, you sign out, or you change your password.
  • Server logs — a short rolling window for diagnostics.

Your choices

In the app you can edit your profile, delete individual transcriptions, revoke microphone or notification permission at any time through your device settings, and close your account outright.

Closing your account erases it. Your profile, your entire library, your sessions, and your registered devices are deleted. This cannot be undone. Because it is irreversible, we ask you to confirm it is really you first — by re-entering your password, or by signing in with Google again if that is how you use NoteFusion.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export, or restrict the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to privacy@notefusion.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Traffic runs over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Access tokens are short-lived and refresh tokens rotate on every use, so a stolen token has a narrow window. Closing your account or changing your password invalidates existing sessions.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please tell us at security@notefusion.app before disclosing it publicly.

Children

NoteFusion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app. Continuing to use NoteFusion after a change means you accept the revised policy.
