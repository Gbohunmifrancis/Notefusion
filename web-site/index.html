How it works

From recording to notation in three steps

Record or import

Tap the mic and sing, or hold the phone up in the rehearsal. If you already have the audio, import a file from your phone.

We work out the music

Pitch, rhythm, tempo, time signature and key, all worked out on our own servers. Each result comes back with a confidence score so you know what to double-check.

Read it and share it

Get Tonic Solfa and staff notation side by side. Play it back, save it, or send it to the team.

Tonic Solfa

Movable-do, done properly

Most transcription tools stop at a stave. NoteFusion derives every syllable from the key it detected, which is what makes the solfa usable by a choir that has never read a note of staff notation.

  • Doh follows the key, across all 24 keys, major and minor
  • Minors are la-based, so A minor reads l t d with doh on C
  • Chromatics get their proper syllables: de, ra, fe, se, ta
  • Octave marks included, so the line is unambiguous to sing from

Frequently asked

Questions we get asked

Do I need to read music to use it?

No. That is what Tonic Solfa is for. If you can sing doh re mi, you can read what NoteFusion gives you. The staff notation is there for when you need to hand it to someone who does read.

How accurate is it, really?

A clean solo voice or a single instrument in a quiet room transcribes very well. A full band in a live room is harder, and dense polyphony is harder still. Every result carries a confidence score, so check it before you teach from it.

What happens to my recordings?

The audio is transcribed and then deleted from our servers: usually within seconds, always as soon as the job finishes. No person listens to it, it is not sold, and it is not used to train anything. See the privacy policy.

Can I change the key after transcribing?

Yes, and it is instant. Changing key re-labels the solfa; it never re-analyses the audio.

Is my transcription mine?

Yes. What you record and what you save belongs to you. Your library is private to your account, and nothing you transcribe is published anywhere.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android first. The app is built cross-platform, so iOS follows. Join the list at hello@notefusion.studio and we will tell you when it lands.
