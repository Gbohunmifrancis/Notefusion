Core Engine

Movable-do, done properly.

Most tools stop at a stave. NoteFusion derives every syllable from the key it detected, making the notation immediately singable for choirs and vocalists who read Tonic Solfa.

  • Doh follows the key across all 24 keys, major and minor.
  • La-based minor mode: A minor reads l t d with Doh naturally anchored on C.
  • Proper chromatic syllables: Sharp and flat notes get their true vowels: de, ra, fe, se, ta.
  • Octave marks included so pitch registers are unambiguous to rehearse from.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to read music to use NoteFusion?

No! That is the core benefit of Tonic Solfa. If you can hum or sing doh, re, mi, you can read and teach what NoteFusion gives you immediately. The staff notation is included for instrumentalists or choir directors who prefer standard notation.

How accurate is the transcription?

A clear hummed or sung line, or a single instrument in a quiet room, comes out close to note-perfect. A full song from a link depends on how cleanly the lead vocal separates from the band: solo voices and hymns are excellent, dense mixes less so. Every note carries a confidence mark so you know what to check.

What happens to my voice recordings?

Your audio is transcribed and then permanently deleted from our servers immediately after the ML worker completes. No person listens to your recordings, and your audio is never stored or used to train third-party models. See our privacy policy.

Can I change the key after transcribing?

Yes, and it is 100% instant. Re-keying is a pure musical re-labelling: Doh updates to the new pitch without re-analyzing or altering the audio fidelity.

Do I need the app, or does it work in the browser?

Everything works in the browser at notefusion.studio/app: links, uploads, recording, the four parts, rehearsal playback, transposing, sharing and downloads. The Android app adds recording in the rehearsal room and an offline library, and uses the same account.

What does Pro include?

Whole songs from a link (up to six minutes, twenty a month), alto, tenor and bass written under every tune, sing-along marking, clean PDF, MusicXML and MIDI downloads, and a priority lane so your job never waits behind someone else’s. Free accounts get one one-minute song preview, unlimited hummed tunes, and sheets they can save and share.

Who owns my transcriptions and notation?

You own everything you transcribe. Your library is encrypted and private to your account. You can export your sheet music as MusicXML or PDF whenever you choose.

How can I permanently delete my account or data?

You can delete your account and all data at any time from within the mobile app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or directly through our public web portal at notefusion.studio/delete-account.
