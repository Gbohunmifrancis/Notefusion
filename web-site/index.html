How it works

  1. Hum, record or upload

    A tune hummed into the mic, a recording of your choir, or a file from your device. You can paste a song link too.

  2. Get the sheet

    The lead voice is isolated and written out: Tonic Solfa, staff notation, key and tempo, with alto, tenor and bass under it.

  3. Rehearse, transpose, share

    Play it back, mute your part and sing against the others, move the key to suit your singers, and send the choir a link.

Movable-do, done properly

Most tools stop at a stave. NoteFusion names every note from the key it hears, so the sheet is ready for choirs and singers who read Tonic Solfa.

  • Doh follows the key across all 24 keys, major and minor.
  • La-based minor. A minor reads l t d, with doh on C.
  • Chromatic syllables named the hymn-book way: de, ra, fe, se, ta.
  • Octave marks on every note, so the register is never in doubt.

Questions

Something else? Email hello@notefusion.studio.

Do I need to read music to use NoteFusion?

No. That is the point of Tonic Solfa: if you can sing doh, re, mi, you can read and teach what NoteFusion gives you. The staff notation is there for instrumentalists and choir directors who prefer standard notation.

How accurate is the transcription?

A clear hummed or sung line, or a single instrument in a quiet room, comes out close to note-perfect. A full song from a link depends on how cleanly the lead vocal separates from the band: solo voices and hymns are excellent, dense mixes less so. Every note carries a confidence mark so you know what to check.

What happens to my voice recordings?

Your audio is transcribed and then permanently deleted from our servers as soon as the job finishes. No person listens to your recordings, and your audio is never stored or used to train third-party models. See our privacy policy.

Can I change the key after transcribing?

Yes, instantly. Changing the key only moves doh: the notes are relabelled, nothing is transcribed again.

Do I need the app, or does it work in the browser?

Everything works in the browser at notefusion.studio/app: links, uploads, recording, the four parts, rehearsal playback, transposing, sharing and downloads. The Android app adds recording in the rehearsal room and an offline library, and uses the same account.

What does Pro include?

Whole songs from a link (up to six minutes, twenty a month), alto, tenor and bass written under every tune, sing-along marking, clean PDF, MusicXML and MIDI downloads, and a priority lane so your job never waits behind someone else’s. Free accounts get 3 hummed or recorded transcriptions every week (back every Friday at 6pm West Africa Time), and the sheets they can save and share.

Who owns my transcriptions and notation?

You own everything you transcribe. Your library is encrypted and private to your account. You can export your sheet music as MusicXML or PDF whenever you choose.

How can I permanently delete my account or data?

At any time, in the mobile app (Settings, Account, Delete Account) or on the web at notefusion.studio/delete-account.
