About the app

What NoteFusion is for

NoteFusion is a free-to-start music transcription app for Android. You record a song with your phone, sing or play it yourself, or import an audio file you already have. NoteFusion listens to it and writes the music down for you in two forms: movable-do Tonic Solfa (d r m f s l t) and standard staff notation you can export as MusicXML or MIDI.

It works out the key, the tempo and the time signature on its own, so you do not need to know them, and it can move a piece into a different key in one tap without recording anything again. It is built first for the music other transcription tools were never trained on: African choruses, worship songs, Afrobeat and highlife. Recordings are analysed on our own servers and deleted as soon as the transcription finishes. We keep the notation, never the audio.

It is for choir masters, worship teams, music teachers and self-taught musicians who need a part written down before the next rehearsal.

Signing in with Google

NoteFusion offers Google Sign-In so you do not have to remember another password. It is optional: you can create an account with an email address and a password instead. When you do choose Google, this is everything we receive and everything we do with it.

That is the whole list. NoteFusion does not ask for your contacts, Gmail, Drive files, photos or calendar, it never posts anything on your behalf, and none of it is sold or shared. The privacy policy has the full detail, including how to delete your account and everything in it.

African Mode

Built for the music nobody else transcribes

Every transcription tool on the market was trained on Western classical and pop. Point one at a Yoruba chorus, a highlife guitar line or an Afrobeat log-drum and it falls apart.

African Mode is tuned for exactly that material: dense percussion, call-and-response, and vocal-led choruses. It is aimed at the songs a congregation already knows and nobody ever wrote down.

  • Nigerian and pan-African church choruses, in the keys they are actually sung in
  • Afrobeat and hip-hop, with the melody lifted clear of the drums
  • Viral sounds and local instruments, from talking drum to shekere
  • Vetted transcriptions published free under African Tunes & Choruses

Frequently asked

Questions we get asked

Do I need to read music to use it?

No. That is what Tonic Solfa is for. If you can sing doh re mi, you can read what NoteFusion gives you. The staff notation is there for when you need to hand it to someone who does read.

How accurate is it, really?

A clean solo voice or a single instrument in a quiet room transcribes very well. A full band in a live room is harder, and dense polyphony is harder still. Every result carries a confidence score, so check it before you teach from it.

What happens to my recordings?

The audio is transcribed and then deleted from our servers: usually within seconds, always as soon as the job finishes. No person listens to it, it is not sold, and it is not used to train anything. See the privacy policy.

What exactly is African Mode?

A transcription mode tuned for material the standard models handle badly. It only changes the analysis. The solfa rules are the same in every mode, with doh following the key and minors la-based.

Can I change the key after transcribing?

Yes, and it is instant. Changing key re-labels the solfa; it never re-analyses the audio.

Is my transcription mine?

Yes. What you record and what you save belongs to you. If you choose to submit a chorus to the public African Tunes & Choruses library, it is reviewed for accuracy first, and you are credited. Nothing is published without you asking for it.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android first. The app is built cross-platform, so iOS follows. Join the list at hello@notefusion.studio and we will tell you when it lands.
