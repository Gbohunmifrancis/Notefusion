Sing it Pro

Pick your voice, hear the starting note and a count-in, then sing into your microphone. Every note comes back marked: in tune, close, or off. Headphones help; nothing is stored.

Green in tune · amber close · red wrong note · grey not sung. Numbers are cents off.

Lyrics

Split syllables with hyphens (a-ma-zing); each goes under the next note. Use grace_ to hold a syllable over two notes. The Tonic Solfa tab shows the hymn-book layout.
