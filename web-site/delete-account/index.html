Data Safety & Deletion Disclosures

At NoteFusion, your privacy and control over your personal data are paramount. Under Google Play Data Safety requirements and global privacy regulations (GDPR / NDPR), you have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

What will be permanently deleted

  • Account & Profile: Your full name, registered email address, authentication credentials, and account profile settings.
  • Music Transcriptions: All saved Tonic Solfa transcriptions, solfa syllables, staff notation scores, audio pitch analyses, and chord sheets.
  • Audio & Voice Recordings: Any temporarily buffered or saved audio clips recorded through the app.
  • Sessions & Tokens: All active login tokens, device push notification registrations, and OAuth grant connections.

Data retention exceptions

If you have an active or previous paid subscription (e.g. NoteFusion Pro), anonymized financial payment transaction logs (e.g. Paystack / Google Play transaction references, invoice records, and statutory tax receipts) are retained strictly as required by applicable tax, commercial accounting, and anti-fraud laws. All associated personal profile identities and musical files are completely scrubbed.

Retention timeline

Account deletion requests initiated with your password or confirmed via email are processed immediately. Your account access will be terminated right away and all database records and storage objects will be irreversibly removed.

Deleting From Within the Mobile App

If you currently have the NoteFusion app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account directly without email verification:

  1. Open the NoteFusion app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Account & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Confirm your decision. Your account and data will be removed instantly.
