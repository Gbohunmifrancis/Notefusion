# Google Play listing

The text the Play Console listing carries (Grow › Store presence › Main store listing), kept here so a change to it is
reviewed like any other. Written from what people type into Play and Google (October 2026: "tonic solfa", "tonic solfa
notes", "convert audio/song to tonic solfa", "hum to sheet music", "sing to notes app", "choir parts", "hymn solfa") and
from the one-star reviews of the apps beside us (a 20-second free taste, ads, trials that charge, wrong keys). Every claim
below is checked against the code; change one only with the feature.

Play's limits: title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. No "free", "best", "#1", "new" or a
price in the title (Play's metadata policy); no other app named anywhere.

## Title (29)

NoteFusion: Tonic Solfa Notes

## Short description (71)

Hum, sing or paste a song link and get its tonic solfa and sheet music.

<b>Hymns</b>

## Replying to reviews

Every review gets a reply within a day, from a person, in plain words: thank the 5-star ones in a line; for anything
lower, say what we changed or will change, and invite them to write from Profile › Send feedback. Play shows the reply under the
review, and people read the replies to low ratings before installing.
